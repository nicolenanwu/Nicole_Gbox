01.

About

I started as an engineer, then spent two years as a field engineer running drilling operations — where the consequences of incomplete information, unclear handoffs, and bad decisions were immediate.

An MBA from INSEAD pulled me toward the business side of hard problems. Since then, I've built across hardware, enterprise SaaS, global operations, and data and AI platforms. I am most at home in the messy middle: where technical constraints, customer needs, operational reality, and business ambition have to come together — not just in a strategy deck, but in something that actually works.

Today, I build products and operating models around trusted data, applied AI, and the systems that help teams make better decisions and scale with confidence.

Outside of work: two early-stage consumer brands, a Private Pilot License earned in 2025, an Airstream, and a Border Collie named Hera, who has strong opinions about hiking pace.
