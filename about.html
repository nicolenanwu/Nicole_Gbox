ABOUT ME

The short version

From oil fields to roadmaps

Engineer, oil-field engineer, INSEAD MBA, product leader, founder, and Private Pilot — roughly in that order.

HOW I GOT HERE

Engineer first, always

I started as an engineer, then spent two years as a field engineer running drilling and measurement operations for Schlumberger — a job that meant working rigs across the US and Canada and getting comfortable making calls under pressure, far from a desk.

That experience pulled me toward the business side of hard problems, which is what took me to INSEAD for my MBA. From there I moved through oil & gas product marketing, into hardware new product introduction, then enterprise SaaS, and eventually into the data and AI platform work I do today.

The common thread the whole way through: I like being close to the messy, technical middle of a problem, not just the slide about it.

Earning my wings

I got my Private Pilot License in 2025 and fly regularly out of Northern California. Learning to fly meant learning to sit with fear, stay calm under pressure, and trust a checklist — habits that have quietly rewired how I approach risk and systems at work too.

I still think in checklists. It turns out that's a pretty good way to run a data governance program as well as a pre-flight walk-around.

RISK & SYSTEMS

What flying taught me about product work

  • Slow down for the pre-flight checklist — every time, no exceptions.
  • Calm under pressure is a skill you practice, not a personality trait.
  • The instruments matter more than your gut when visibility is low.

LIFE OUTSIDE WORK

Hera, hot pot, and the open road

Our Border Collie, Hera, joins almost every trip — including the Airstream run up and down the West Coast in 2024. She has opinions about hiking pace and none about my cooking.

Speaking of which: I cook a lot, and I take hot pot very seriously wherever I travel — ask me for a recommendation in almost any city and I probably have one.

I'm a native Mandarin speaker with professional fluency in Spanish, which comes in handy running two import/e-commerce brands and generally getting around.
