Notes from a product and AI leader using modern tools to plan trips and share what is useful on the ground.

Considering a trip to Oklahoma City? These are a few favorite spots, documented as part of an AI-assisted travel planning and storytelling experiment.

Oklahoma is home to gambling

Oklahoma offers a wide range of gaming experiences, from slot machines and table games to poker and bingo. With over 100 casinos across the state, it is a major part of the local economy. Our favorite is Riverwind Casino for strong blackjack and poker table options. There is also an on-site hotel, and membership can unlock occasional complimentary room offers.

You wouldn't believe this is Oklahoma

Tellers is one of the most distinctive dining spots in Oklahoma City. Set in a restored historic bank, the atmosphere is memorable and the food quality is strong. If you want one standout dinner stop, this is an easy recommendation.

Garden in Winter

Oklahoma City's botanical garden is calm, well maintained, and worth visiting year-round, including winter. The slower pace and cleaner views make it a good reset between busier stops in the city.

Hot Pot in OK?

If you are in Oklahoma City and want authentic hot pot, 99 Flavor BBQ & HotPot is a strong pick. It is newly opened and serves flavorful broth without MSG.
